ACSA Safety Culture & High Reliability Organization (HRO) Demonstration & Evaluation Project​

The Alberta Construction Safety Association (ACSA) is leading an industry-wide Demonstration & Evaluation Project focused on evaluating a more effective approach to contractor performance assurance. Intactix Systems serves in a project management and implementation role, with academic support provided through Athabasca University.

It is a practical industry initiative demonstrating and evaluating whether a Shared Performance Assurance Framework can help create the foundation for an industry-wide safety culture that integrates safety into everyday work while reducing administrative burden across the contractor ecosystem.

Toward A More Effective Approach to Contractor Performance Assurance

Across industry, organizations have invested enormous time, resources, and leadership attention into improving safety performance.

New programs, initiatives, contractor management systems, verification processes, and performance assurance requirements have become standard practice.

Yet despite these efforts, industry continues to face the same uncomfortable realities:

  • Industry has struggled to meaningfully reduce serious incident rates.
  • Similar incidents continue to repeat.
  • Contractors continue to experience disproportionately high rates of serious injury and fatality.
  • Administrative burden continues to increase for both Contractors and Hiring Clients.

Industry is looking for practical solutions that simplify rather than add. The challenge is no longer effort. The challenge is results.

Contractors and Hiring Clients ultimately want the same outcome: safe, reliable, and predictable operational performance.

Yet that outcome is often pursued through different systems, different measures, different expectations, and different contractor management processes.

Industry has recognized that performance assurance is a shared challenge that requires a shared approach.

A Demonstration of a Different Approach

This is not another safety requirement. It is a practical methodology designed to provide objective visibility into the factors that create operational performance.

Grounded in applied behavioural science, sociotechnical risk science, and High Reliability Organization (HRO) principles, the project is utilizing a socio-technical methodology that operationalizes safety culture and operational reliability, providing a new approach to measuring performance that:

  • Does not rely on employee surveys, interviews, or self-reported perceptions
  • Minimizes administrative effort and disruption to normal operations
  • Creates objective visibility into safety culture and operational performance
  • Supports continuous performance assurance through ongoing monitoring

Building on demonstrated results achieved within individual organizations, the initiative is evaluating whether a Shared Performance Assurance Framework can provide the common performance language, shared expectations, and measurable High Reliability Organization (HRO) performance needed to support an industry-wide safety culture. The objective is to achieve:

  • Reduced Serious Injury and Fatality (SIF) Exposure
  • Stronger Operational Performance
  • More Effective Performance Assurance
  • Less Complexity and Burden
